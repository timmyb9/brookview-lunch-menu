Privacy Policy — Kitchen Display Calendar

Kitchen Display Calendar is a private, self-hosted family wall display. It is not a public service.

It requests read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) solely to show the household's own calendar events on a display inside the home. Event data is fetched on demand, stored only on a private home server, and is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or any other purpose.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
